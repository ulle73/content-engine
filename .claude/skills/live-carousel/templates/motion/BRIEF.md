## Intent

A "live carousel": an Instagram carousel where every slide is a motion-graphics
video with sound effects, so people swipe and see every slide move. Topic: motion design.
The hook has to be the craziest part; the 4th slide is the CTA.

Concept: THE BOUNCING BALL, the first exercise every animator learns. One orange ball
travels through all four slides (it leaves slide 1 at the right edge and enters slide 2 from
the left, so swiping feels continuous), and each slide teaches with it.

1. HOOK: "your posts are STATIC." The frame looks dead still (0 FPS). The ball crashes in and
   hops across the letters, the word flips to "ALIVE.", the beat drops. The ball leaves to the
   right ("swipe"), the beat tape-stops, and the word goes back to STATIC. Seamless loop.
2. RULE 01, EASING: "never move LINEAR." Linear vs eased ball side by side, graph-editor curves.
3. RULE 02, SQUASH & STRETCH: "give it WEIGHT." The ball lands on the word; the type squashes too.
4. CTA: "comment MOTION": the ball becomes the O; a comment bar types MOTION and posts it.

## Customizations

- House carousel style: one dominant cobalt colour,
  orange accent, cream type, "small line / BIG WORD / small line" stack, top-bar microtype,
  small-caps bottom triplet.
- A timeline ruler at the bottom runs across all four slides like one long animation timeline.
- 120 BPM synthesized beat + sound effects. Each slide is a whole number of bars, so the
  audio loops cleanly on Instagram as well.

## Notes

- 1080x1350 (4:5), 30 fps, one MP4 per slide plus a local swipe preview page.
- No handle or Instagram branding baked in. The CTA copy ("I'll DM you the prompt") can be swapped.
