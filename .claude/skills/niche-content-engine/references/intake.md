# Intake

Ask these in ONE message. Keep it friendly and short. Mark the first three as the ones that
matter; everything else is optional.

```
Quick setup so I can find what's actually working in your niche:

1. What do you do, and where? (e.g. "residential roofing in Dallas")
2. Who's your ideal customer? (e.g. homeowners 35–65, property managers, insurance claims)
3. Pick one:
   A) Send me 1–5 creators you like (links or @handles), or
   B) Send me your own account / a few videos that did well, or
   C) Just tell me what you want to talk about.

Optional — makes the scripts sharper:
4. Something you can give away for a comment (checklist, price guide, free inspection, a PDF)?
5. Anything true about your business I can use? (years in business, jobs done, a real story,
   a typical price range, your guarantee)
6. Filming style: talking to camera, on the job site, voiceover over footage, or a mix?
7. Anything off-limits? (topics, competitors, pricing you won't share)
```

## Defaults when they skip something

| Missing | Default |
|---|---|
| Customer | The most common buyer for that trade in a home/consumer setting |
| Creator/topic input | Do the research sweeps only; pick topics from what's viral |
| Giveaway | Propose one per script from the list in `formats.md` → "Deliverables" |
| Business facts | Write lines that don't need them; never invent them |
| Filming style | Talking to camera, with B-roll suggestions |

## Remember across the conversation

Once you have the answers, reuse them for every later request in the same chat. Don't ask again.
Keep a running list of comment keywords you've used so none repeat.
