# Formats that work for service & local businesses

Pick the format that fits each finding. Examples use a roofer; swap in any trade.

| Format | Hook shape (first 3s) | Body | Words | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Red flags** | "If your {trade} says this, walk away." | 3 red flags, one line each, what to say instead | 100–140 | trust, "don't get scammed" |
| **What I'd never do** | "I've been a {trade} for X years. Here's what I'd never do in my own home." | 3 things + why, one pro detail each | 110–150 | authority |
| **Price reveal** | "Here's what a {service} actually costs in {city} right now." | what drives the price, range (only the user's real numbers), what's a rip-off | 100–140 | leads — highest intent |
| **Is this normal?** | "Is this normal? [shows photo/damage]" | what it is, how bad, what to do | 80–120 | reaction, comments |
| **Myth vs truth** | "Everyone thinks X. That's wrong." | the myth, the truth, the proof on camera | 90–130 | shares |
| **Signs you need it** | "3 signs you need a new roof before the next storm." | 3 visible signs viewers can check today | 90–130 | leads |
| **Job-site story** | Starts mid-action: "We pulled this shingle off and found…" | the problem → what it would've cost → the fix | 100–150 | trust, watch time |
| **Before / after** | "This roof was 2 weeks from caving in." | the problem, the process in 3 beats, the result | 60–110 | visual proof |
| **DIY vs call a pro** | "You can fix this yourself. This one, you can't." | one DIY fix, one "call someone" line | 100–140 | helpfulness, saves |
| **Timely / news** | "{City} just got hit with hail. Here's what to check today." | 3 checks, what insurance usually needs (general), CTA | 90–130 | urgency |
| **Verdict list** | "Metal roof: worth it. Tile: depends. 3-tab shingles: …" | one-clause verdict each, best last | 50–120 | comments, debate |
| **Answer a comment / question** | "Someone asked me: …" | direct answer + one surprise fact | 80–120 | series, community |
| **Day in the life / POV** | "POV: you're a {trade} in 110° heat." | fast montage, one real lesson at the end | 40–90 | relatability, reach |

## Mixing rule (per batch of 5)

- At least **2 lead formats** (price reveal, signs you need it, red flags, timely).
- At least **1 trust/story format** (job-site story, what I'd never do, before/after).
- At most **1 timely/news** item, and only if a finding from this week supports it.
- No format used more than twice. No two scripts on the same sub-topic.

## Deliverables (for comment-keyword CTAs)

"Comment WORD and I'll send you ___" works because the viewer gets something real. Only promise
what the user can actually send. Good defaults they can make in 10 minutes:

- Checklist / inspection sheet (e.g. "10-point roof check you can do from the ground")
- Price guide or "what should be on your quote" sheet
- Questions to ask before hiring a {trade}
- Seasonal prep list
- Insurance claim photo checklist (general guidance, not legal advice)
- Free inspection / consultation (if they offer it)

Keyword = one short, memorable word tied to the deliverable: ROOF, QUOTE, STORM, CHECK, PRICE.
Never reuse a keyword in the same batch or one the user already used.
