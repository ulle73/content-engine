```
## What's working in {niche} right now
- **{Finding}** — {one-line why}. [source]({link})
- ... (5–8 bullets: outliers, audience pain points, this week's hook, creator patterns)

## Your 5 ideas
| # | Title (the hook) | Format | Why it'll work | Keyword |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ... | Price reveal | 40 Reddit threads this month asking ... | QUOTE |

## Scripts
(one block per idea, exactly as in script-rules.md)

## Filming notes
- B-roll to grab on your next job: ...
- Post in this order: 3 → 1 → 5 → 2 → 4 (lead with the strongest hook, space out formats)
- Deliverables to make before posting: ...

---
Which ones do you want reworked — or should I go again with a different angle?
```

Keep the whole thing scannable: bold the hooks, short bullets, no long paragraphs of strategy.
