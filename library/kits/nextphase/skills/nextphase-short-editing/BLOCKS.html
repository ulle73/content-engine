Library · every animation

The blocks, live.

Everything animated in the library, old and new, running — not screenshots of it. A still of an animated block is the most common complaint, and a stale still also freezes whatever the plate looked like the day it was shot, which is how the retired graph-paper grid stayed on screen for weeks after it was killed. These iframes always show the block as it is now. Every block takes ?seek=<s> and is rendered to mp4 with scripts/block-render.py before it goes in a cut.
