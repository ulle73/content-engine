zak

A rounded window floating on pure black, with real air above and below it. One serif line in the air, set once. Two-to-four-word captions inside the window that replace themselves on a single frame. Nothing moves.

What breaks it

a shadow on the caption Measured: there is none. If the type is hard to read the PLATE is wrong, not the type. scripts/check-contrast.py.
a drift, push-in or parallax That is motiongraphics. Here the camera is bolted down.
a one-word caption That is persa. Zak captions are phrases.
an asymmetric window The air above and below is equal to the pixel. Off-centre reads as a mistake, not a choice.
a drop shadow under the window There is nothing behind it to catch a shadow. It is invisible work.
a second accent colour There are two colours in this style: black and white.
