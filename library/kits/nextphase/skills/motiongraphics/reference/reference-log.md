Everything in this skill was derived from two edits, sampled at 1fps for structure and
at 30fps across every transition. This file is the evidence: when you need to know
*exactly* how a beat was done, find it here rather than guessing.

- **REF-A** — `youtube.com/watch?v=vwlK6MbdAto` · studied 0:00–3:10 · 1920×1080 30fps
- **REF-B** — `youtube.com/watch?v=xkj74ykB9Fw` · studied 0:00–0:50 · 1920×1080 30fps

Timecodes below are from the start of each video.

## Measured palettes

Sampled from saturated pixels (S > 0.40, V > 0.35) across key frames.

**REF-A** — violet `#843CF0` `#9048FC` `#7830E4` · deep `#5418B4` `#3C1884` `#300C6C` ·
field indigo `#241860` · coral `#D87854` · ground `#080808`–`#101018`.
The coral is within 1–2 points of NextPhase's existing `--coral: #D97757`, so the two
house styles already share a brand accent.

**REF-B** — magenta `#A824CC` `#9C0CCC` · highlight `#C054E4` `#CC6CF0` · mid purple
`#843CA8` `#9048B4` · deep `#481860` `#54246C` · ground `#000000`–`#100018`.

Both live on the same violet→magenta ramp at different temperatures, which is why one
token set covers both. Cyan values sampled in REF-A (`#0C3C60`, `#009CB4`) are **set
lighting in the room**, not graphic tokens — do not put them in a composition.

## Measured materials

- **Node pill** (REF-A 0:51 at 3× zoom): fully rounded, fill `--mg-panel` barely lifted
  off the ground, **2px lavender stroke** `#A99CF5`, outer glow *and* inner glow, white
  medium grotesque label with generous tracking. Connectors are ~2px lavender bezier
  curves with a glow, joining pill edge to pill edge in a smooth S.
- **Glass tile** (REF-B 0:42 at 3× zoom): saturated violet-magenta fill, **2px white
  rim brightest along the bottom edge** (lit from below), radius ≈ 8% of the tile,
  a flat purple pictogram with a darker outline above a **light-weight** white label,
  soft dark shadow plus a bloom halo around the whole tile.
- **Media card**: 3px near-white border, `--mg-r-card` radius (36px on the phone
  variant), rim glow plus a deep shadow. Real media is *always* an object on a stage.

## Corrections log

Two beats were re-studied at higher sampling after the first pass read them wrongly.
Both errors were the same *kind* of error — reading a continuous camera move as a set of
discrete element animations — which is worth remembering as the failure mode:

1. **Diagram takeover.** First read as "the stack re-fits when a panel is added"
   (discrete, `--mg-dur-refit`). Re-sampled at 2fps across the full 30s: it is **one
   unbroken camera pull-back**, with the title landing large. Also missed: the third
   panel entirely, and the grey→colour arrival of each document.
2. **Object stage.** First read as "three tiles in a row with a dolly". Re-sampled at
   7.5fps: it is a **two-act scene** — a rim-lit figure with translucent tiles arriving
   at scattered depths, then a solid row the camera **dives along laterally**. Also
   missed: that the two tile treatments are lit from opposite directions.

When a scene looks static in a 1fps contact sheet, sample it at 7.5fps before describing
it. Camera moves disappear at low sampling rates and read as "nothing is happening".
