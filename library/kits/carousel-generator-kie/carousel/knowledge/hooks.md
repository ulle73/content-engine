# 04 — Hooks (slide 1)

The hook decides whether anyone swipes. Make a big, specific promise or a surprise.
Emphasize ONE word in the accent color (the "hero word").

## Formulas (fill in the blanks)
- **Before → after:** "[Big task] used to take [time]. Now it takes **[tiny time]**."
- **Surprising result:** "An [person] did [surprising thing] with [tool]."
- **The new best:** "The new [category] is here: **[name]**."
- **Contrarian:** "Stop [common approach]. Do **[better thing]** instead."
- **Number lead:** "**[N]** [things] that [benefit]."
- **Curiosity gap:** "Here's what [person] did — and why it **works**."

## Good vs weak
- ✅ "Half a day of work, **now minutes**" (specific, concrete)
- ❌ "AI is changing accounting" (vague, no promise)
- ✅ "The new smartest Claude is here: **Opus 5**"
- ❌ "A new model dropped" (no stakes)

## Tips
- Put the payoff in the FIRST line, not buried.
- Keep it to ~6–9 words on the main line + the hero word.
- The subtitle (pill) adds the one-line context: who/what.
- Match the hook image to the world (from your style sheet), keep the top open for text.
