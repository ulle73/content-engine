# 03 — Carousel structure

**Keep it to 5–7 slides.** One idea per slide. The arc:

| Slide | Role | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **HOOK** | Stop the scroll. Biggest promise or most surprising line. Everything rides on this. |
| 2–(n-1) | **BODY** | One idea per slide: the setup → the how → the proof/result. No walls of text. |
| n | **CTA** | One clear action: comment a word, follow, save, try it. |

### Rules that make it work
- **One idea per slide.** If a slide has two thoughts, split it.
- **Front-load value.** Slide 1–2 should already deliver something useful.
- **The middle can't be empty.** Put a chart, a stat, a real screenshot, or a mascot in the middle band — never blank background.
- **Accent word.** Exactly ONE glass capsule per slide (`hero_word`). Never two, never coloured text.
- **Consistent world.** All backgrounds come from the SAME style sheet so the set feels like one piece.
- **Keep it black.** At least 40% of every slide is unbroken void black; the light only ever comes
  from inside the scene.

### Two proven shapes
**Research/data topic (e.g. a product launch):**
1 Hook → 2 The claim/scorecard → 3 A benchmark → 4 Price/value → 5 A standout stat → 6 CTA.

**Story/how-to topic (e.g. the accountant):**
1 Hook (result) → 2 What they did → 3 The old way → 4 How it works now → 5 The payoff (before→after) → 6 Caveats → 7 CTA.
